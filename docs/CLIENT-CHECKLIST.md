# Owner-defined crossover checklist

Approved 14 November 2026. The 45 prompts below are review metadata. Canonical Sales CRM fields, document labels, signed snapshots and IDs are unchanged. Each Checked answer needs appropriate attached source evidence and a page/section reference. Not Applicable requires a genuinely inapplicable item and a recorded reason.

| Section | Review prompts, in order | Canonical source / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client and Site Details | Legal Name; Location, Suburb, Postcode; Contract Signed; Email Present; Contact Given | Legal Name, Location, Suburb, Postcode, Signed Contract Status, Signed Contract Date, Sale Submission History, Email 1, Contact 1. Identification and/or Electricity Bill cross-compared with the verified Signed Contract. |
| Site Details | Site Type Check; System Type Check; Phase Check; Roof Check; Height Check; NMI Check; Building Type; Strata Managed; Inspection Done | Site Type, System Type, Phase Type, Roof Type, Height, NMI, Building Type, Strata Managed, Site Visit, Remote Site Visit, Site Visit Notes, Remote Visit Notes. Requested/scheduled inspection is not completed; remote evidence needs manual review. Torrens Title permits a reasoned Strata Managed Not Applicable. |
| Grid and Design | Grid Approval Done; PV Design Approved; Battery Location Approved; EV charger Location | Grid Status, Grid Provider, NMI, Approval Number, Approval Date and Grid + Meter File. Existing PV Design / Battery Location labels. EV plan uses Other with an explicit location/approval reference. Design/location checks require reviewing approval evidence, not merely uploading a file. |
| Product Check | PV Match; Battery Match; Racking Match; Extra Product Match | Current signed products, every specification/model/quantity and repeated line. PV includes panels and associated inverters when PV was sold. Battery-only inverters remain covered by STC and Inventory product schedules. Extra products include Cabinet Storage / EV Charger when sold. |
| Inventory and STC Check | STC Correct; STC Electrician Correct; STC Product Correct; STC Client Details Correct; STC Site Details Correct; System Size Correct; Battery Size Correct; Panel Product Match; Inverter Product Match; Battery Product Match; Racking Type Match; Extra Order Match | First five belong to STC; last seven to Inventory. Real exports/screenshots attached as Other, cross-compared with verified Signed Contract, Electricity Bill or separate authorised job instructions. Inventory source status, exact current products/quantities, physical packing and release criteria remain required independently. |
| Pricing and Extras | Total Price Correct; OFP Correct; Extra Price Match; Deposit Correct; Balance Price Correct; Extra Given; Extras Match | Total Price, OFP Balance, Extra Balance, Deposit; Final OFP Balance = OFP Balance + Extra Balance. Show OFP Pay Mode, Payment Notes and finance fields together. Extras retain signed scope, quantities and amounts and organised arrangement metadata. First five belong to Pricing, final two to Extras. |
| Notes and Documents | Mandatory Document Match; Other Document Match; Site Instructions Clear; Final Review by Admin | Signed Contract, Identification, Electricity Bill, plus PV Design / Battery Location when sold. Review all remaining actual attachments individually. Site Instructions / Rep Advice remains canonical. Final Admin review is explicitly last and requires every other assessment to pass or have valid Not Applicable. |

The internal bar uses the seven section headings in the table, in that exact order, and opens only each matching list. The front register and backend release checks are preserved. Client and Site Details, Site Details, and Notes and Documents reflect Order approval; Inventory and STC Check requires both Inventory and STC; Pricing and Extras requires both Pricing and Extras. Grid and Product map directly. Overall progress counts these seven visible sections. The source viewer remains dominant and ends at the document. Operational Completion Details are collapsed within the selected checking panel.

The attachment strip shows four cards across at wide desktop sizes and scrolls horizontally for additional files. Narrow screens use fewer visible cards. The open attachment has a dark Petrol background, Viewing label and accessible selected state; opening another file moves the highlight.

Separate comparison files and references are stored on the version-specific answer. One file cannot masquerade as both the STC export and the independent signed order, or as both STC electrician details and authorised job instructions. Missing bytes, unrecognised files and fictional examples cannot be evidence. The application records manual review; it does not perform OCR or prove source content automatically.

No canonical assigned-electrician source or EV location master field was found in the existing local Sales schema. Do not substitute STC Creator or invent a Sales column: require manual documentary comparison for now. STC/Inventory integrations and automated product reconciliation remain pending.

Schema 3 invalidates earlier checklist rounds without rewriting their items, findings or scores. Operational source edits also invalidate the current round. Grid/design, STC, Inventory and final Admin attestation are excluded from rep coaching; coverage is tracked separately. The seven backend approvals, Yes confirmation, version invalidation and 24-hour queued reminder rules remain enforced. This checklist does not deliver messages or book installation.
